Our Story

For nearly seventy years, our family business has supplied homes across Pakistan with genuine electronics and home appliances. What began as a single shop in Peshawar in 1956 has grown into 17 showrooms — and now this online store, so every Pakistani home can shop the same trusted range from anywhere.

What We Sell

Air conditioners, refrigerators, LED TVs, washing machines, freezers, geysers, air coolers, kitchen appliances and much more — from the brands Pakistan trusts: Haier, Dawlance, Samsung, TCL, LG, Kenwood, Super Asia, Gree, Orient, EcoStar and many others.

Our Promise

Every product we sell is 100% original and covered by the official manufacturer warranty. We offer free delivery across Pakistan, honest prices, easy payment options — cash on delivery, Easypaisa, JazzCash and bank transfer — and friendly after-sales support on call and WhatsApp.
